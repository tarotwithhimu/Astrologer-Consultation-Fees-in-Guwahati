## Why Do Astrology Consultation Fees Differ?

Many people compare astrologers only by price. However, the fee alone does not necessarily tell you how detailed or useful a consultation will be.

Several factors can influence the **astrologer consultation fees in Guwahati**.

### 1. Experience of the Astrologer

Experience is one of the factors that can influence consultation pricing. An astrologer who has spent years studying astrology and interpreting different types of birth charts may structure consultations differently from someone offering a very basic horoscope reading.

Experience can also matter when a consultation involves complicated subjects such as marriage timing, career changes, business decisions, relationship issues, or multiple planetary periods.

### 2. Consultation Duration

A 15–20 minute consultation and a 45–60 minute consultation cannot reasonably be expected to provide the same level of discussion.

Short consultations are generally useful when you have one or two focused questions.

Longer consultations allow more time for:

* Birth-chart analysis
* Planetary placements
* House analysis
* Dasha considerations
* Relevant transits
* Multiple life areas
* Follow-up questions
* Practical guidance

Therefore, when comparing **astrologer fees in Guwahati**, always compare the duration and scope of the consultation rather than looking at the price alone.

### 3. Depth of Horoscope Analysis

A basic horoscope reading may focus on general tendencies. A detailed consultation can examine the horoscope from multiple angles.

For example, someone asking about marriage may require analysis of the seventh house, seventh lord, Venus, Jupiter, relevant dashas and planetary influences. Similarly, career questions may require examination of career-related houses, their lords, planetary placements and timing factors.

The more detailed the analysis, the more time may be required.

### 4. Type of Question

Not every consultation requires the same approach.

A person asking, "What does my horoscope indicate about my career?" may require a broader analysis.

Someone asking, "Why am I experiencing repeated problems in my relationship?" may need a consultation focused specifically on relationships.

Likewise, questions about marriage, education, business, finances, relocation or family matters may require different areas of the birth chart to be examined.

## What Information Do You Need for an Astrology Consultation?

For a traditional birth-chart-based consultation, the astrologer generally needs accurate birth information.

This commonly includes:

**Date of Birth:** Your exact date of birth.

**Time of Birth:** As accurate as possible. Even differences in birth time can sometimes affect chart calculations, particularly when house positions or divisional charts are being considered.

**Place of Birth:** The city or location where you were born.

You should provide the most accurate information available to you.

If your birth time is uncertain, tell the astrologer instead of presenting an estimated time as exact. In some circumstances, astrologers may discuss birth-time rectification or work with the available information.
