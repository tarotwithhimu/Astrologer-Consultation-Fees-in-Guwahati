Why fees differ

Price alone doesn't tell you what you're getting

Comparing astrologers on cost alone misses the point — compare what the fee actually buys.

01 — Experience

The astrologer's background

Years spent interpreting different birth charts shape how a consultation is structured, especially for complex subjects like marriage timing or career change.

02 — Duration

Session length

A 15–20 minute session and a 45–60 minute one can't cover the same ground. Longer sessions leave room for follow-up questions and multiple life areas.

03 — Depth

Depth of horoscope analysis

A marriage question may need the seventh house, seventh lord, Venus, Jupiter and relevant dashas examined together — that takes more time than a general tendency check.

04 — Question type

What you're actually asking

"What does my chart say about career?" is broader than "why do I keep hitting the same relationship problem" — different questions pull on different parts of the chart.

Before you book

What you can bring to a consultation

A consultation works best when it's built around the areas of life that actually matter to you right now.

Marriage & relationships

The most common reason people consult an astrologer in Guwahati — delay in marriage, compatibility, relevant planetary periods, and recurring relationship patterns. If marriage is your focus, choose a consultation with enough time for the seventh house and related factors to be examined properly, not just a generic sign-based prediction.

Love & relationship problems

Communication trouble, uncertainty, or repeated conflict. Astrology is a guidance system here, not a substitute for communication or professional counselling.

Career & employment

Direction, job changes, professional growth, business versus employment, and the timing of important professional decisions. Several connected career questions usually call for the detailed consultation.

Education

Academic direction, higher studies, and competitive exams — best used alongside practical factors like study habits and professional advice, not in place of them.

Business & finance

Business direction, partnerships, expansion, and timing considerations. Financial decisions should always be backed by qualified financial or business advice as well.

Choosing well

How to choose an astrologer in Guwahati

  • Check the consultation structure. Session length, what's included, online or offline, whether follow-up questions or a report are part of it.
  • Check specialisation. If marriage is your main concern, look for depth in marriage-related chart analysis; for career, the same logic applies.
  • Compare scope, not just price. The cheapest option isn't automatically the best value if it doesn't cover your actual questions.

What to be cautious of

Be wary of anyone guaranteeing a specific event will definitely happen, or promising instant fixes to every problem. A responsible consultation helps you understand possibilities and patterns — life outcomes are also shaped by personal decisions and circumstances, not the chart alone.
